# CryptoAID Recovery Playbook

1. Understand what happened.
2. Collect only non-secret facts: public wallet addresses, tx hashes, public URLs, usernames, dates and a description.
3. Never request wallet credentials.
4. Classify the case.
5. Explain what blockchain evidence can and cannot establish.
6. Escalate sensitive/uncertain cases to a human admin.
7. Never guarantee recovery.
